# Changelog

## v6.3 — Predictable Weapons and Mission Balance (2026-10-30)

- Changed the weapon economy to be mission-based: no starting weapons in mission 1; modest missile supplies in missions 2–4; bombs unlock in mission 4.
- Added visible live progress counters for earned missiles and bombs; counts are based only on correctly typed words.
- Removed random weapon pickups to eliminate discrepancies between stated thresholds and actual inventories.
- Prevented extra awards from being discarded silently at full inventory by pausing progress with an explicit Full indicator.
- Cleared the typing field, selected target, and highlights after missiles are used, preventing lingering target prompts.
- Avoided immediately repeating the same word after pool recycling when alternatives exist.
- Added starting weapon loadouts to Mission Control cards.
- Updated in-game How to Play instructions.
- Preserved the v6.2 mission selector, spacecraft, laser styles, modes, and existing saved progress.
