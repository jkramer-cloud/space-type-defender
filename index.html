How to play

Type the word attached to a meteor. The word is accepted as soon as its final letter is typed. Press Backspace to correct. In Arcade mode you have three lives. Relaxed mode is endless until you finish your run. Higher levels are faster and require more words. Nova bombs: press Enter or the Bomb button to clear all visible meteors. You start with 2, can hold at most 3, and earn 1 for every 5 words you type correctly. Some glowing supply meteors also award a bomb. Bomb-cleared words do not count as typed words. Letters on the selected falling word turn green for correct typing or red for mistakes.

Word categories unlock gradually as missions progress. There are no bears or category-switching controls in Space Type Defender.

Accuracy and speed are practice indicators. Words are teacher-curated by broad grade band, not an official list required by Washington state.
