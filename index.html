How to play

Type the word attached to a meteor. The word is accepted as soon as its final letter is typed. Press Backspace to correct. In Arcade mode you have three lives. Relaxed mode is endless until you finish your run. Higher levels are faster and require more words. Custom spacecraft: open Ship Hangar to choose from eight designs and six laser effects. Precision missiles: press Tab or click Missile to destroy your current target; if no word is selected, it destroys the closest approaching meteor. Supplies depend on the mission. Mission 1 starts with none, and each reward is earned by completing the displayed number of words. Nova bombs: press Enter or Bomb to clear all visible meteors. Bombs unlock at mission 4. Harder missions give a small starting supply, and the HUD shows exactly how many words remain to earn more. Random supply meteors are disabled so word counters remain predictable. Neither weapon awards typing points or word-completion credit. Letters on the selected falling word turn green for correct typing or red for mistakes.

Word categories unlock gradually as missions progress. There are no bears or category-switching controls in Space Type Defender.

Accuracy and speed are practice indicators. Words are teacher-curated by broad grade band, not an official list required by Washington state.
